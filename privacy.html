Privacy Policy

Effective 13 September 2026. Matchbook is a lightweight football information app with no account system and no advertising cookies in this build.

Plain-language summary: Matchbook combines public/free football sources and does not require a private sports API key. It has no private user database, and optional analytics stays off unless a valid analytics ID is configured and you explicitly consent.

Information the app processes

Search terms, navigation choices and game guesses are processed so the interface can work. Matchbook does not include account registration or user profiles. Football searches and data requests may be sent through the site's serverless data gateway to third-party public football sources.

Third-party football data

Matchbook combines TheSportsDB, Football-Data.co.uk and OpenLigaDB. The serverless /api/football gateway may request team, squad, standings, result and fixture data from those services and cache responses temporarily. Some TheSportsDB player/profile requests can also be made directly from the browser. Those providers and the hosting platform may receive ordinary network information such as IP address, request time and browser/network metadata under their own policies.

Images and licensing

Club badges and player pictures are resolved through Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons. Matchbook only displays a Commons image when returned metadata identifies it as public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA, and the displayed image links to its Commons file page for attribution. Free copyright licensing does not necessarily remove trademark, personality/publicity or other non-copyright restrictions.

Analytics, cookies and local storage

Advertising cookies are disabled. Google Analytics support is included but ships without a Measurement ID, so it sends no analytics data by default. If the site owner adds a valid Measurement ID, Matchbook asks for consent before loading Google Analytics. Your analytics choice is stored locally in your browser under matchbook-analytics-consent. Declining analytics does not block the app.

Security and “background secrets”

Anything embedded in client-side HTML or JavaScript can be inspected by visitors, so passwords, database credentials, signing keys and private API secrets must never be placed there. This build does not require a private football API credential. TheSportsDB's documented public free v1 key (123) is not treated as a secret.

Retention and caching

Matchbook does not provide an account/profile database. Football responses may be cached temporarily by the serverless/CDN layer to reduce repeated requests to free providers, and ordinary hosting logs may be retained according to the hosting provider's settings. In-memory browser results disappear when the page is closed or refreshed. The optional analytics-consent choice remains in local storage until you clear it or use the reset control above.

Your choices

You can decline optional analytics, clear the site's local storage, block third-party requests with browser privacy tools, or stop using the app. If the deployed site later adds advertising, accounts, forms that submit personal data, or another analytics provider, this policy should be updated before those features go live.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top should be revised whenever a material change is made.
